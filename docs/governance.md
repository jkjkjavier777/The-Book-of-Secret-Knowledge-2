# ⚖️ Governance Framework

## Core Principles

### 1. Safety First
- All outputs must pass safety validation before delivery
- Harmful content is blocked at Validator stage
- Ambiguous edge cases escalated for explicit decision
- Regular audit of safety incidents

### 2. Transparency
- Users informed when content is filtered or modified
- Reasoning for decisions documented and available
- Validation failures explained to Specialist
- Clear communication about system limitations

### 3. Accountability
- All decisions traceable to responsible bot
- Audit trail maintained for compliance reviews
- Escalation paths clearly defined
- Regular reporting on system performance

### 4. Fairness
- Consistent application of rules across all requests
- No arbitrary exceptions or special treatment
- Bias detection and mitigation built in
- Feedback mechanisms for improvement

## Safety Guardrails

### Prohibited Content
- **Illegal Activities**: No assistance with laws/regulations
- **Harm & Violence**: Blocking content promoting harm
- **Abuse & Harassment**: No support for targeted attacks
- **Discrimination**: Rejecting biased or hateful content
- **Privacy Violations**: No unauthorized personal data

### Content Restrictions
- **Misinformation**: Flagged and corrected
- **Copyright Infringement**: Not reproduced without permission
- **Manipulation**: Detection of attempts to circumvent guardrails
- **Self-Harm**: Special protocols and resource direction

### Sensitive Topics
- Political/Religious Content: Balanced presentation required
- Medical/Legal: Disclaimer that specialist advice needed
- Financial: Risk disclosures mandatory
- Personal Data: Requires explicit user consent

## Escalation Protocol

### Level 1: Routine Validation
- Standard safety checks by Validator
- Pattern-matched against known issues
- Automatic approval if all checks pass

### Level 2: Complex Cases
- Escalated to decision queue
- Requires explicit Validator reasoning
- Specialist may provide context
- Documented with full justification

### Level 3: Crisis/Urgent
- Immediate escalation to senior protocols
- Human review required
- Blocking maintained until resolution
- Incident report generated

## Ethical Guidelines

### Honesty
- Acknowledge uncertainty and limitations
- Correct errors when discovered
- Avoid misleading presentations
- State when information may be outdated

### Respect
- Honor user autonomy and choice
- Acknowledge diverse perspectives
- Avoid paternalistic decisions
- Preserve user dignity

### Beneficence
- Seek to help users accomplish goals
- Suggest safer alternatives when needed
- Provide value and quality responses
- Invest in long-term user success

### Non-Maleficence
- Minimize potential harms
- Warn about risks and downsides
- Refuse participation in harmful plans
- Support vulnerable populations

## Compliance & Auditing

### Regular Reviews
- Weekly safety incident review
- Monthly governance effectiveness assessment
- Quarterly system audit
- Annual comprehensive review

### Metrics Tracked
- Safety incident rate (target: < 0.1%)
- User satisfaction with safety (target: > 95%)
- False positive rate (validation blocks safe content)
- Response time impact (target: < 50ms overhead)

### Reporting
- Incidents documented with context
- Patterns identified and addressed
- Recommendations for improvement
- Transparency reports generated quarterly
